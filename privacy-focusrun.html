Privacy Policy for Focus Run

Effective Date: October 17, 2026

TP Digital ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Focus Run application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application.

Our Commitment to Privacy

Focus Run is designed with a local-first architecture. All your focus sessions, performance metrics, timer events, disruption tags, and historical records are stored locally on your device using an embedded SQLite database.

Information Collection and Use

  • Local Data Storage: All data generated while using Focus Run—including focus durations, breaks, disruption logs, and timestamps—remains strictly on your device. We do not collect, transmit, upload, or store your personal focus analytics on any external servers.
  • No Personal Data Harvesting: We do not require account registration, login credentials, or personal identifiers (such as name, email address, or phone number) to use the core features of the App.
  • Diagnostics and Updates: Depending on where you downloaded the App (such as the Google Play Store or Microsoft Store), the respective platform provider may collect standard anonymous diagnostic data and crash logs in accordance with their Terms of Service.

Third-Party Services

The App utilizes standard platform services for app distribution, updates, and optional premium unlocks (where applicable), which operate under their own respective privacy policies. We do not share any user data with third-party advertisers or analytics networks.

Data Security

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you maintain total ownership and control. You can clear or delete all recorded session history at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing its app data through your system settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this section with an updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our developer profile or support channels under TP Digital.
