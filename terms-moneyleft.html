2. Description of the Application

Money Left is an offline-first, local-first financial ledger, expense tracker, and budget management utility developed by TP Digital.

Key operational characteristics of the Software include:

  • Local Storage: All financial records, ledger transactions, recurring payment rules, project budgets, and custom categories are stored solely in a local database (SQLite) on your own device.
  • Local Network Sync: Multi-device synchronization operates strictly peer-to-peer (P2P) across your private Local Area Network (Wi-Fi or LAN) without intermediate cloud servers.
  • No Cloud Custody: TP Digital does not operate cloud databases for your financial transactions, does not require user registration or logins, and does not hold custody of your financial records.
