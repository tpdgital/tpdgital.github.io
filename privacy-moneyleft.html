Privacy Policy for Money Left

Last Updated: November 22, 2026 Publisher: TP Digital Platforms Covered: Android and Windows (Desktop)

1. Introduction & Core Philosophy

At TP Digital, we believe your financial data is deeply personal and should remain strictly confidential. Money Left is engineered from the ground up as a 100% local-first, offline-first personal and business ledger management application.

Whether you use Money Left on Android, Windows PC, or both, this Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled, stored, and protected.

Our Fundamental Promise:

  • Zero Cloud Custody: We do not operate cloud databases or external financial storage servers. Your financial data is never uploaded to, inspected by, or stored on TP Digital servers.
  • Zero Account Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email, phone number, or personal identity to use Money Left.
  • Zero Tracking & Zero Ads: We do not embed advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, data brokers, or marketing trackers.

2. Information Storage & Local Architecture

All data you input into Money Left—including expenses, income, recurring schedules, category allocations, project ring-fencing budgets, remarks, and receipt tags—is stored locally on your device in a structured, local SQLite database and local application storage.

  • On Android: Data is stored within the app’s sandboxed private storage directory.
  • On Windows: Data is stored in your user-profile application data directory and user-designated local backup folders.

At no point does TP Digital possess access, decryption keys, or visibility into your ledger records.

3. Local Network Peer-to-Peer Synchronization (LAN / Wi-Fi)

Money Left includes a multi-device synchronization engine that enables a Windows PC Hub to coordinate with linked Mobile Companion devices.

  • Local Network Only: Synchronization operates strictly peer-to-peer (P2P) over your private Local Area Network (Wi-Fi or LAN).
  • No Cloud Relays: Data payloads exchange directly between devices on your local network (default local port 8080). Sync data never transits through cloud servers, remote relays, or external proxies.
  • Pairing Authentication: Devices pair using a transient 4-digit PIN code or local QR code containing local LAN IP and device pairing tokens.

4. Data Ownership, Independence & Software Disclaimer

  • 100% User Ownership: You retain sole, exclusive ownership and legal custody of all data entered into the application.
  • Independent Records: Money Left is strictly a record-keeping software tool. Neither Money Left nor TP Digital audits, certifies, verifies, or holds legal association with the transactions, accounts, or ledger data maintained by any individual or commercial entity.
  • Statements & Document Bearings: Reports, PDF statements, and spreadsheets generated or printed by Money Left bear the software application name and branding solely to identify the tool used to format the document. They do not indicate that the financial transactions, assets, or liabilities belong to, or are endorsed by, Money Left or TP Digital.

5. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

Money Left only requests permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver its offline and local-sync capabilities:

Permission Platform Purpose
Camera Android Used solely to scan the local desktop pairing QR code. Video or images are never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
Local Network / Wi-Fi Android & Windows Used exclusively to discover and transfer sync data between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi / LAN.
File System / Storage Android & Windows Used strictly to export reports (PDF, Excel, CSV) and create or restore offline backup archives (.mlbak) at your direction.
Notification (Optional) Android Used to alert you of due recurring payments or scheduled reminders set by you.

6. Payments & Licensing

  • Android (Google Play): Optional Pro subscription upgrades are processed securely by Google Play Billing (Google LLC). Financial transaction details (e.g., credit card numbers, billing addresses) are handled directly by Google under their respective Privacy Policy and are never visible to or stored by Money Left.
  • Windows PC: Inherits Pro capabilities directly from a licensed mobile companion device over local synchronization or via local verification without external tracking.

7. Backups, Data Portability & Complete Deletion

  • Manual & Automated Offline Backups: Money Left offers local backup snapshots (.mlbak). These portable backup archives reside exclusively on your local file system or user-chosen folder.
  • Complete Data Deletion: Because all data is stored locally, you have total autonomy over its removal.
    • To wipe all data on Android, select Clear Data in Android Settings or uninstall the app.
    • To wipe all data on Windows, use the in-app Reset / Clear Data option or uninstall the software.
    • Deleting the app immediately and permanently erases all locally stored database files.

8. Children’s Privacy

Money Left is a general-purpose productivity and accounting tool that does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect application improvements or regulatory changes. The latest version will always be accessible directly within the application settings and our source repository.
